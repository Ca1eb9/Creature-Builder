## Fonts — SIL Open Font License 1.1

Both typefaces are used under the OFL. The license requires its text to be
distributed with the fonts, so the full text lives beside the `.ttf` files in
`Assets/Fonts/` and is included in builds.

| Font | Copyright | License text |
|---|---|---|
| Cormorant Garamond | Copyright 2015 the Cormorant Project Authors (github.com/CatharsisFonts/Cormorant) | `Assets/Fonts/OFL-CormorantGaramond.txt` |
| Lora | Copyright 2011 The Lora Project Authors (github.com/cyrealtype/Lora-Cyrillic), with Reserved Font Name "Lora" | `Assets/Fonts/OFL-Lora.txt` |

Two OFL conditions worth remembering:

- The fonts may be bundled and sold **as part of** this software, but never
  sold on their own.
- A **Reserved Font Name** (Lora) may not be used for a modified version. If a
  font is ever edited, the derivative must be renamed.
